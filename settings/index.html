Pets

Screens pets can use

    A pet you can see asks in its own balloon. If you don't answer in time, the pet puts the question away and wanders again; click it to see the question.

    Without those keys, pets move out of the way of your mouse and of where you're typing, and clicks go straight through to your windows. A question waiting for your answer can always be clicked.
